NAME:
FT Expand All Comments

SUMMARY (≤132 chars):
Expand or collapse every comment on a Financial Times article in one click — nested replies and "load more" included.

DESCRIPTION:
FT Expand All Comments adds a one-click control to Financial Times articles that
opens — or collapses — the entire comment discussion at once.

WHAT IT DOES
• Expand all: opens every collapsed thread and keeps clicking "show more replies"
  / "view N replies" / "load more comments" until the whole discussion is on screen,
  then stops.
• Collapse all: returns the thread to a clean, collapsed baseline.
• Always Expand mode: automatically expands threads as the page loads (and respects
  any comment you collapse yourself — it won't fight you).

BUILT TO BE INVISIBLE
• Does nothing on FT pages without a comment section — no stray button, no console noise.
• Survives FT's lazy-loaded, dynamically-mounted comment widget and single-page navigation.
• Bounded and safe: never hangs on a stuck "load more", never loses your place.

PRIVACY
• Collects no data, sends nothing to any server, has no account and no tracking.
• Works only on www.ft.com, and only acts when you click or with Always Expand on.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

activeTab:
Lets the toolbar popup talk to the Financial Times article in the current tab only,
on user action, to trigger expand/collapse. No broad tab access is requested.

storage:
Stores a single on/off preference ("Always Expand") so the choice persists across
page loads. No personal data is stored.

Host access (content script on https://www.ft.com/*):
The expand/collapse logic must run inside FT article pages to find and toggle the
comment threads. Scope is limited to www.ft.com; no other site is accessed.
